"Public discourse on emerging high-tech products (humanoid robots, AI agents) elicits a particular kind of compound affect—blending technological awe with replacement anxiety, anthropomorphic empathy with ontological doubt, distancing humor with genuine fear. While sentiment analysis and stance detection have been extensively studied, this specific intersection (compound affect × tech reception × Chinese social media) remains under-explored. We probe 5 affect detection systems on a curated subset of 18k comments and identify systematic failure modes; we discuss why this domain requires moving beyond standard taxonomies."
